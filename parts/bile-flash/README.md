# Bile Flash

The bile jar is a flashbang: no bile, a white flash and a bang; every infected within 500u (witches and tanks too) is blinded, slowed, kept staggering and cannot attack for 5 s.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lversus`, `hd4lsurvival`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/bile_flash.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
