# Arsenal

Weapon changes per mode, no weapon scripts:
- M60: a scattergun, 7 pellets of 20 damage, 125 clip, a pause after every 4 volleys.
- Pistols fire while held, single pistol damage 0.7x; dual pistols fire twice as fast.
- Pitchforks and shovels become fire axes, golden crowbars are removed.
- Blunt melee hits launch commons and push specials. Corpses fly further.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lversus`, `hd4lsurvival`, `hd4lfort`, `hd4lfreebuild`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/arsenal.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
