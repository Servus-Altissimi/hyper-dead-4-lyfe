# Armour

Incoming damage on survivors is cut to 0.65x while moving (200 u/s or more, in the air, or pinned) and to 0.85x standing still. Fall and drown damage are exempt. Called from inside no-incap's hook, so lethality is judged after the cut.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lversus`, `hd4lsurvival`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/armour.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
