# Escalation

The coop director over the campaign: commons 55 to 90, specials 6 to 10, faster respawns. Progress grows by a fifth per map, spread over the first 8 minutes out of each saferoom, and peaks on the fifth map.

- A tank every map after the first (90 to 180 s out of the saferoom), in groups of two near the peak; tanks have 6000 health.
- Finales: one tank at a time, amped (1.5x health, 1.2x speed, glowing eyes); finale pressure eased to 0.6.
- Up to 3 witches per map placed ahead, out of sight.
- Threat spikes at 25, 50, 75 and 100% progress.
- Merciless while anyone is in HYPER: short mob timers, 1.25x specials. Last stand caps pinners and horde size instead.
- Commons capped at 100; the furthest are culled.

**Modes:** `hd4l` only

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/escalation.nut`, `addon/scripts/vscripts/escalation_tank.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
