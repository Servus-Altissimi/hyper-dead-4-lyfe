# Self Defib

A defib in your kit is an extra life: a lethal hit burns it instead, you come back at 50 health plus 25 temp with 1.5 s of invulnerability. Half of the upgrade pack spawns become defibs.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lversus`, `hd4lsurvival`, `hd4lfort`, `hd4lfreebuild`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/self_defib.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
