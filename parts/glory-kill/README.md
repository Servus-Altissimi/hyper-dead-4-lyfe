# Glory Kill

A special below 35% health (a tank below 10%) reels and bleeds for 3.5 s (tank 6 s; versus 1.2 s and 2.5 s). Bot specials go blind and deaf meanwhile.

A dash, heavy push, kick or shove in that window finishes it (a tank only by a kick): gore, a 280u blast that kills commons and staggers specials, a stamina refill and 15 temp health. Bullets in the window are just bullets. A glory finish on a boomer does not puke.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lversus`, `hd4lsurvival`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/glory_kill2.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
