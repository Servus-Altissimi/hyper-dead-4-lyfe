# One Life

The survivors are the lives: no rescue closets, a wipe ends the run (permawipe), and who died carries into the next chapter. A human in a dead character stays dead. Versus: one life per chapter, nothing carries.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lversus`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/one_life.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
