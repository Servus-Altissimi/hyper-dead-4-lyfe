# Fort

Survival as waves with building.

- Prep: everyone has only an axe. Every map item turns into junk worth scrap; any prop that turns amber under the crosshair breaks into scrap over a few hits (by size). Tap walk for build mode: left click places, right click and reload cycle, middle mouse switches list (build, prop, item), look up to build higher, hold reload to take a piece back, hold right click to repair.
- 39 blueprints: wood and grate walls, floors, stairs, roofs and doors on a 128 grid; sandbags, barriers, containers, towers, razor wire, fire barricades, mines, explosives, floodlights, a minigun and a 50 cal; supplies.
- Waves start with the survival button: 90 s, 45 s longer each, a random gun with a laser per wave, tanks from wave 4, more and faster infected every wave. Breaks of 75 s with a scrap bonus and no guns.
- The horde wears pieces down; a piece that loses its support falls; broken pieces become debris that crushes infected. Infected leap up at survivors on the fort; bots left below are lifted up.
- `hd4lfreebuild`: endless scrap.

**Modes:** `hd4lsurvival`, `hd4lfort`, `hd4lfreebuild`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/fort.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
