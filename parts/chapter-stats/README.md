# Chapter Stats

Scores the run: commons 10, specials 150, witches 800, tanks 1500; kick, dash, heavy and glory kills earn a bonus, headshots 5, parries 250, break frees 120. Kills within 3 s chain (+0.1x each, up to 4x). Damage taken and deaths cost points, time under par earns them.

The HUD shows the live chain from 3, plus campaign time, kills and best chain, which carry between chapters. Each player's rank and totals are logged to the console at the end of a chapter.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lversus`, `hd4lsurvival`, `hd4lfort`, `hd4lfreebuild`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/chapter_stats.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
