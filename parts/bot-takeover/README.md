# Bot Takeover

Die and carry on as a bot, no death screen: a lethal hit swaps you into the healthiest free bot (character, health, inventory) and the bot takes your death. A defibbed bot is reclaimed by a dead human first.

**Modes:** `hd4l`, `hd4lsurvival`, `hd4lfort`, `hd4lfreebuild`

**Code:** `addon/scripts/vscripts/bot_takeover.nut`. Knobs sit at the top of each script.
